Privacy policy: Broker Research Reader

Last updated 2 November 2026.

What the app accesses

With the account holder's permission, the app reads one Gmail inbox that receives broker research reports, using Google's read-only Gmail permission (gmail.readonly). It reads messages and their attachments. It cannot send, delete, label or change any mail.

How the information is used

The app records each report's sender, date, company, rating, target price and a short summary, for the research team's own investment research. Report text is processed by Anthropic's Claude AI models to extract these details.

The information is not sold, not shared with anyone outside the team, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is kept

On the team's own computers and private database. Nothing is published.

Removing access

The account holder can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On request, the team deletes what it has recorded.

Contact

Through the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
